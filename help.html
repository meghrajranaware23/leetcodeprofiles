HELP & SUPPORT

We're here to help you stay on your learning path.

How we can help

LeetCode Profiles is built for structured DSA learning. If something isn't working as expected — billing, pack access, progress sync, or your account — reach out and we'll get you back on track.

When to contact support

  • Billing & subscriptions — payment issues, subscription status, refunds, or cancellation questions
  • Access problems — premium packs not unlocking after purchase, or sign-in issues
  • Progress sync — lessons completed but progress not saving across devices
  • Account — profile, email, or account deletion requests
  • Bugs & feedback — broken pages, errors, or suggestions to improve the platform

Contact us

Email our support team and include the email address linked to your account plus a short description of the issue.

leetcodeprofiles@gmail.com

We aim to respond within 1–2 business days. For faster self-service, review our pricing, learning method guide, or privacy policy.

Before you write

  • Check that you're signed in with the same Google account you used to subscribe
  • For payment issues, note whether you paid via PayPal or Razorpay
  • Include your browser and device if reporting a technical bug
